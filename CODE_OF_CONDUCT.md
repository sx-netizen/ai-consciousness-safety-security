# ACSS Code of Conduct

ACSS welcomes rigorous disagreement and good-faith criticism across AI research, philosophy, cognitive science, and security engineering.

## Expected conduct
- Critique claims, evidence, methods, and reasoning rather than people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and respect research participants' privacy.
- Distinguish established findings, exploratory observations, and speculation.
- Respect intellectual attribution and do not misrepresent another contributor's work.
- Report methodological errors and security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable conduct
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, retaliation, fabricated evidence, and disclosure of confidential or sensitive information without authorization are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement
At this founding stage, ACSS has not established an independent conduct committee or confidential reporting channel. Do **not** post sensitive reports publicly in GitHub Issues. Until a suitable private reporting mechanism and independent review process are established, contributors should avoid sharing identifying or confidential details through the repository. The future governance process should define impartial review, recusal, proportionate responses, appeals, and protection from retaliation.

This document describes community expectations; it does not imply that ACSS is already a registered organization or has an operational enforcement body.
