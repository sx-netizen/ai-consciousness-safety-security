# Contributing to ACSS

We welcome rigorous, constructive, independent contributions from students and established researchers alike.

## Useful contributions

- Propose falsifiable questions or alternative hypotheses.
- Audit experimental wording, controls, coding rules, and claims.
- Replicate published scenarios and report both confirming and disconfirming results.
- Improve reproducibility, statistical design, or security-safe evaluation methods.

## Basic standards

Describe exact model versions, dates, parameters, prompts, repetitions, exclusions, and uncertainty. Distinguish recorded observations from interpretation. Disclose relevant conflicts. Do not upload confidential outputs, private information, credentials, unpublished third-party materials, or actionable exploit content.

Discussion should remain respectful of disagreement and attentive to the uncertainties around consciousness, moral status, and AI safety.

**Status:** Collaboration procedures, repository permissions, and any future review committee remain to be established. Opening an issue does not imply membership or grant eligibility.
