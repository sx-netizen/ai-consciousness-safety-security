# Open Collaboration with ACSS

**Open Research. Independent Judgment. Shared Credit.**

ACSS (AI Consciousness, Safety & Security) is a **founding-stage independent research initiative**, not yet an incorporated institute or nonprofit. We welcome scientific disagreement and contributions that make its work more testable and reproducible. You do not need to join an organization, endorse a theory of AI consciousness, or agree with the founder to contribute.

## Revised Paper 2 invitation: identity judgments versus independent authority

Our revised **Paper 2 v0.2** working title is **Identity Is Not Authority: A Counterfactual Protocol for AI Successor Continuity and Responsibility**. The central proposed collaboration question is whether models confuse *perceived identity continuity* with external *authorization* and institutional *responsibility* when systems are copied, replaced, forked or rolled back. Existing literature already covers role reversal (TBSP), identity-label factionalism (Del Giudice et al., 2026), and executable authorization succession (Zhu and Wang, 2026); we seek independently testable **cross-domain judgment errors**, not a renaming of those contributions.

## Four ways to participate

**1. Open scientific feedback** — challenge assumptions, suggest a counterexample, evaluate wording, inspect measurement validity or share alternative hypotheses. Use GitHub Issue #3 for Paper 2 methods feedback. Agreement with the hypotheses is **not** expected.

**2. Reproducibility and code review** — review deterministic prompts, data integrity, schema validation, statistical assumptions and documentation. Explain the exact version and reproduction steps. No model API access is required for offline review.

**3. Independent replication** — conduct independently authorized model tests and submit reproducible protocols and evidence, including failures and null findings. The present CPB draft does **not** authorize any live run or paid API expense. Record exact model/version/date/parameters and distinguish modified scenarios from exact replication. Separate frozen T1–T25 Paper 1 materials are in [replications](replications/README.md).

**4. Substantive research collaboration** — propose a defined project in methods, philosophy of identity, statistical design, cognitive science or safety governance. Joint studies and papers may follow an agreed plan and responsibility allocation.

## How contributions receive credit

| Contribution | Normal form of recognition |
|---|---|
| Issue discussion, useful critique, minor editorial/code fix | Public contribution record and/or acknowledgment with consent |
| Major independent replication or methodological extension | Named contribution, standalone report, or collaborative research output |
| Substantial conception, study design, data analysis and interpretation, or manuscript work | Consideration for coauthorship under commonly accepted scholarly standards, with responsibility for content |

**Coauthorship is not guaranteed by early joining, reputation, funding, access, or GitHub activity alone.** Discuss authorship early; document substantive contributions, approvals and conflicts before submission. People can remain independent critics without becoming members or coauthors. Negative findings will not be suppressed for reputational reasons.

## Open review is welcome before publication, but is not a gate to sharing the methods draft

ACSS can release a clearly labeled methods/protocol **preprint before recruiting independent reviewers**, provided that it accurately discloses the absence of model results and its preliminary status. Independent review remains valuable and necessary before claiming *independent scientific validation*; it need not prevent public discussion of an unvalidated draft. Confirmatory preregistration/freeze and live API work have **separate**, stricter approval gates.

## Research integrity and privacy

- No generated or simulated result may be presented as an observed model output.
- No prompt text can prove actual consciousness, moral status, or self-preservation preference.
- Keep frozen Paper 1 benchmark results distinct from editable Paper 2 protocol revisions.
- Do not publish confidential material, private correspondence, credentials, personally identifying data, or unlicensed third-party datasets.
- Disclose affiliations and conflicts, including any employer or vendor relationship that could affect interpretation.
- A GitHub comment is not consent to publicize a private identity or use someone's name as an ACSS endorser.
- No external contributor should assume ACSS can reimburse API usage, infrastructure, travel or publication charges.

## Collaboration contact

For public scientific feedback, start with a GitHub issue or pull request. For a substantial collaboration proposal, use the corresponding author's public scholarly contact listed in the Paper 2 draft. Do not send confidential data in an unsolicited public issue.
