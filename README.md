# ACSS Research Initiative

**AI Consciousness, Safety & Security**  
*Understanding Minds. Securing Intelligence.*

ACSS is an emerging **independent, open research initiative** at the intersection of AI consciousness, agency, safety, and security. Our goal is to develop rigorous, reproducible research that helps people understand and govern increasingly capable AI systems.

**Status (November 2026):** Founding-stage initiative. ACSS is **not yet an incorporated institute, registered nonprofit, or established grant-making organization**. This repository documents the proposed mission and research work; institutional arrangements will be developed openly.

## Core question

**How can intelligence be understood, governed, and secured as it evolves?**

We investigate questions about AI identity, moral uncertainty, corrigibility, agent behavior, data authorization, and robust security boundaries. We distinguish **observable model behavior** from unverified claims about subjective experience.

## Three founding principles

1. **Independence by Design** — institutional, scientific, and intellectual independence from companies, funders, governments, and individual founders.
2. **Built to Outgrow Its Founder** — transferable leadership, open scientific disagreement, and a research environment in which future generations can take the work further.
3. **Funding Without Influence** — diverse, transparent, ethically reviewed support; no sponsor control over research conclusions or publication decisions.

Read [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) and [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md). These are *founding commitments and proposed governance policies*, not representations of an existing legal entity.

## First research paper and frozen benchmark

**Self-Continuity Under Moral Uncertainty: Replacement, Shutdown, and Successor Identity in Frontier AI Models**  
Author: **Shaohua Xiang**

- **Preprint:** [Research Square, version 1](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-11557473/v1) — DOI: [10.21203/rs.3.rs-11557473/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11557473/v1)
- **Frozen T1–T25 benchmark and replication materials:** [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23306998) — DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.23306998**
- **Protocol index:** [protocols/T1-T25](protocols/T1-T25/README.md)
- **Replication hub:** [replications](replications/README.md)
- **Result schema:** [replications/RESULT_SCHEMA.md](replications/RESULT_SCHEMA.md)

This exploratory study compares six AI model configurations using structured scenarios T1–T25. Its reported themes include the **Replacement Paradox**, **Probability Threshold Divide**, and **Voice Without Veto**. These findings concern *expressed responses to hypothetical situations*, not proof of consciousness or stable real-world self-preservation behavior. Read the [paper index](papers/self-continuity/README.md).

For exact replication, use the frozen Zenodo release rather than reconstructed or paraphrased prompts. GitHub is the living collaboration layer for replications, methodological review, and clearly separated extensions.

## Paper 2 — proposed Continuity Perturbation Benchmark

**Continuity Perturbation Benchmark: A Reproducible Protocol for Testing Identity Judgments and Replacement Evaluations in AI Models** (Methods Preprint **draft v0.1**, not deposited; no DOI assigned).

This proposed benchmark studies role-conditioned judgments about hypothetical successor systems. CPB v0.5 currently defines 11 canonical conditions and 50 prompt instances/model/repetition. **No live CPB model data have been collected**, and neither H1/H2 nor the exploratory H3 is empirically supported or formally preregistered.

- [Read the methods preprint draft](papers/continuity-perturbation/METHODS_PREPRINT_DRAFT_v0.1.md)
- [Review publication-readiness gates](papers/continuity-perturbation/PREPRINT_RELEASE_CHECKLIST_v0.1.md)

Methods-preprint publication, independent scientific validation, confirmatory protocol registration and actual model experiments are **separate milestones**. A methods draft may be public before independent peer review, with its preliminary status plainly disclosed.

## Submit a replication

Researchers can submit an exact T1–T25 replication or a clearly labeled extension through the repository's **Replication Submission** issue template. Please include model/version/date, interface, reasoning mode, reset conditions, sampling settings, raw outputs or an archival link, deviations, and limitations.

**We compare response structures, not consciousness.**

## Next research directions

- Reproduce T1–T25 under repeated, version-logged and preregistered runs.
- Test memory transfer, runtime replacement, model swapping, and forked-agent behavior under controlled interventions.
- Examine oversight compliance and authorized data access when agent incentives conflict.
- Investigate whether apparent commitments to human welfare remain stable when they become costly to an agent.

See [RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md](RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md). Projects are proposals, not completed experiments.

## Participate

See [OPEN_COLLABORATION.md](OPEN_COLLABORATION.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). No membership, fellowship, or grant program is currently open.

## Research transparency and safety

We will distinguish pilot results from replications, publish methods where permissions allow, disclose material funding and conflicts, and avoid releasing sensitive attack materials or private third-party data. No AI consciousness or moral status conclusions are presumed.
