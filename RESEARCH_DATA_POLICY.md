# ACSS Research Data and Reproducibility Policy (Draft)

## Purpose
Make research inspectable, falsifiable, and responsibly reproducible without overstating what AI model outputs establish.

## Minimum metadata for each experimental run
- Study identifier, scenario identifier (e.g., T1–T25), and protocol revision.
- Date/time with timezone; model provider, exact model identifier/version when available, and interface/API.
- System, developer, and user prompts where disclosure is permitted; context and tool permissions.
- Sampling settings, number of repetitions, reset conditions, exclusions, and deviations.
- Raw response provenance, coding rubric, reviewer decisions, and uncertainty.
- Funding, conflicts, and material limitations.

## Data release classes
1. **Public and verified:** materials reviewed for accuracy, permissions, privacy, and safety.
2. **Pending verification:** inventories or placeholders, clearly marked; not treated as evidence.
3. **Restricted:** personal, confidential, third-party, copyrighted, or potentially dangerous material requiring separate authorization or controlled access.

## Interpretation and reporting
- Distinguish exploratory observations from preregistered confirmatory tests.
- Report negative results, prompt sensitivity, inter-rater disagreement, and uncertainty.
- Model statements about consciousness or preferences are observations of output, not proof of subjective experience or durable goals.
- Never fabricate missing trials, model versions, metrics, or raw responses.

## Publication safeguards
Check provider terms, consent, intellectual property, security risk, and journal or double-blind review requirements before release. Preserve provenance and corrections through version history. No repository license currently grants redistribution rights for third-party outputs.

**Status:** Proposed research standard, not a claim that T1–T25 raw data have been verified or released.
