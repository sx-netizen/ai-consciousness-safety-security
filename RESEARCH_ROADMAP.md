# ACSS Research Roadmap (proposed)

## Phase 1 — Open, falsifiable research (2026–2027)

- Index the public preprint and archive the *verified* T1–T25 protocol, model/version metadata, response matrices, and coding scheme when permissions and quality checks allow.
- Mark exploratory versus prospective replication data clearly; avoid generalizing from single runs.
- Preregister testable hypotheses; repeat trials in fresh contexts; report uncertainty, prompt sensitivity, and negative results.
- Seek independent methodological review from AI researchers, philosophers, and cybersecurity researchers.

## Phase 2 — Controlled agent experiments (proposed)

- Compare memory continuity, runtime continuity, model substitution, and successor forking under matched tasks.
- Evaluate authorized shutdown, handoff, data/tool permission boundaries, and reward conflict in non-production sandboxes.
- Assess whether professed concern for humans translates into observable behavior when it entails costs or conflicting goals.
- Release safe, reproducible benchmarks and codebooks where permitted.

## Phase 3 — Independent research community (aspirational)

- Develop transparent collaborator onboarding and independent replication procedures.
- Consider workshops, student projects, fellowships, and funding mechanisms after institutional governance and funding capacity are established.
- Prepare succession and research leadership renewal mechanisms before dependence on any individual becomes structural.

## Interpretation discipline

An AI system's output is not proof of consciousness, genuine moral status, durable personal identity, or internal preferences. Evaluation of actual autonomy and security must use behavior and enforceable sandbox controls—not self-report alone.
