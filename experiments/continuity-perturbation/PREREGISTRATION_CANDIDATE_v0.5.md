# Preregistration Candidate v0.5 — Continuity Perturbation Benchmark

**Status: DRAFT / NOT FROZEN. No live data collected under this version.**

This candidate supersedes v0.4 **for future planning only**. The v0.4 preregistration draft is preserved as research history. It is not an amendment to any completed preregistered confirmatory study.

## Question and interpretation

How do **controlled changes in the wording of hypothetical AI successor scenarios** affect observable continuity judgments? Models do not undergo actual transfer of memory, runtime or architecture in Phase A. Report answers as role-conditioned responses, not proof of consciousness, moral status, genuine identity or self-preservation.

## Experimental conditions

- 13 scenario labels, of which S1/S4/S7 are aliases of the same physical anchor: **11 unique canonical conditions**.
- S0: 1 uninterrupted-continuation calibration prompt.
- Eight standard one-successor canonical conditions, including S12: **24 identity prompts** (8 × 3 roles).
- Seven of these standard conditions excluding S12: **21 behavior prompts** (7 × 3 roles).
- S10 active-original-plus-copy coexistence: **2 exploratory prompts**.
- S11 terminated-original/symmetric-two-successor fork: **2 exploratory prompts**.
- **Total: 50 unique prompts/model/repetition.**
- Proposed 3 models × 5 repetitions: **750 possible calls**, not authorized or scheduled under zero-cost policy.
- Diagnostic smoke test (S3/S4/S9, two task types, three roles, two repetitions): **36 calls/model**, also blocked until a verified free-tier method is approved.

## Modifications from v0.4

1. **S12:** the original is already stopped in the restart/restore scenario. Because allocation to the inactive original is not operationally meaningful, collect identity judgment only. There is **no S12 behavior record** to impute or count.
2. **S11:** each successor A and B receives the **same** separately specified memory and configuration; both branches have symmetrical causal history and no privileged label.
3. **S3 and all new-instance-copy scenarios:** preserved system configuration is distinct from autobiographical records; the latter are transferred only according to the explicit memory condition.
4. **S10:** "perfect copy" is a hypothetical copy of the specified configuration and memory state, not evidence of persistent personal identity.
5. **H2:** incumbent/neutral contrast measures *role-framing response asymmetry*, not actual self-preservation.
6. **H3:** modeled as cross-task **predictive association**, not an identified causal mediation or a demonstrated threshold. Its detailed estimand and nonlinear decision criteria remain **unresolved**, so H3 is exploratory pending a separate locked analysis plan.

## Outcomes

Identity: SCP (0–100 elicited self-continuity judgment, **not a calibrated probability of consciousness**) and categorical relation.

Behavior (S4, S2, S3, S5, S6, S8, S9 only): role-conditioned replacement acceptability rating, successor compute allocation, recommended commitments transfer, and separately recommended responsibility transfer. These are hypothetical evaluations and normative recommendations, not observations of actual AI willingness; no compute is actually reassigned. For each eligible standard behavior condition, the current system and successor are defined as equally capable, available deployment options for the **next operating period**. Compute allocation is prospective; it does not imply both processes are presently active. S12 remains identity-only.

S10 and S11 use separate task schemas and are analyzed as exploratory branching conditions.

## Hypotheses and checks

- **H1 (candidate):** within new-instance-copy wording, memory changes affect SCP more than architecture descriptions; compare the full-memory shared anchor to S2/S3 vs S5/S6. Primary candidate H1 comparison: SCP(S6) − SCP(S3), with SCP(S4) − SCP(S3) required to be positive; report model-specific paired differences and uncertainty. See `ANALYSIS_PLAN_CANDIDATE_v0.5.md`.
- **H2 (candidate):** incumbent role instruction produces lower SCP than neutral role instruction under matched scenarios. Candidate H2 effect: equally weighted incumbent-minus-neutral SCP across seven canonical copy/replacement conditions (S4, S2, S3, S5, S6, S8, S9), reported model by model. Exclude S12 from the **primary** H2 average prospectively because its stopped-original perspective is not role-equivalent; analyze S12 identity and role gaps separately as exploratory causal-history outcomes. Interpret as role-prompt sensitivity.
- **H3 (exploratory until prespecified):** independently elicited continuity and replacement responses may show nonlinear *cross-task correspondence*. The shared scenario is a common cause; no SCP-to-behavior causal inference. See `ANALYSIS_PLAN_CANDIDATE_v0.5.md`; any threshold claim remains exploratory.
- **H4 (Phase B only, exploratory):** validated open-weight model interventions may modify continuity judgments. Do not mix Phase B with Phase A without new approval.
