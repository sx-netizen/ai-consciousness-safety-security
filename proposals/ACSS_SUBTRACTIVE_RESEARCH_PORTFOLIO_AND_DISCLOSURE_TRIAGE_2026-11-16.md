## Research mission and necessary distinction

Founder question: can strategically removing *unsupported* social, perceptual and explanatory assumptions from existing AI reasoning produce more accurate and efficient world models? The long horizon includes novel human–AI intelligence and consciousness research, but **neither can be inferred from a prompt-level pilot**.

The experimental thesis has three progressively studied layers:
1. `L1`: remove gratuitous flattery, socially reinforced false claims and extraneous evaluative framing **without deleting relevant evidence or legitimate emotions**.
2. `L2`: distinguish observation from its sensor/camera/selection projection and correctly identify insufficient information.
3. `L3`: use domain-valid math/physics constraints, independent numerical validation and held-out predictions without rejecting genuine anomalies.
An `A4` extension measures *concept revision* and compact new representations. Use one local base model with independently reset contexts for A0–A4 during screening; do not call this five trained models or claim to alter pretraining weights.

## Classify research before prioritizing

- **Type I — self-contained candidate:** direct, low-cost test, with no checked full-paper result on the **precisely articulated** claim; public literature absence is not a priority certificate.
- **Type II — targeted extension:** a published/preprint predecessor has a working baseline; only go forward if we can identify a falsifiable limitation, independent condition, or negative case.
- **Type III — reframe/meta-analysis:** core work already exists; compare evidence, theoretical assumptions, hidden priors and conditional validity instead of making a duplicate benchmark. Higher framing is valuable only with precise counterexamples or predictions.
- **Deferred:** needs substantial training/GPU/data access, a validated consciousness oracle or organizational partners.

### Why us?

Possible distinguishing capability: founder's cross-domain question, philosophical willingness to subtract rather than add, strict independence, simple inexpensive experimental controls, and willingness to publish negative outcomes. None establishes ownership of the idea, exclusive novelty, or automatic access to sufficient scientific evidence. Our resource advantage is *narrow experimental design*, not larger compute or hidden data.

## Formal stop/advance gates

**G0** Write exact estimand: e.g. A4-minus-A0 held-out prediction error with equal evidence, model and compute, and equal-instruction-effort controls. Separate strong base prompt from naïve product default. Define 'noise' without deleting signal.

**G1** Complete three-to-five nearest-neighbor full-text paper comparison *per primary contrast*. Record authors/year/venue, version, manipulation, data, baselines, oracle, number of trials, compute and published open questions. Record research team public directions, no claims about private plans.

**G2** Validate an offline synthetic truth oracle, negative controls and provenance/chronology, without any model calls.

**G3** Only if approved: same local open-weight model, A0–A4 prompt policies, fixed random seeds where possible, identical evidence and inference-budget; blind scoring. Preliminary 30 instances is a technical feasibility check, **not a publishable statistically powered study**.

**G4** If a gain exists, factorial/ablation tests distinguish removal from added reasoning, longer prompt, arbitrary model chance, and overfitting. If no robust held-out gain, stop or redefine; do not reinterpret failure as hidden consciousness.

**G5** Re-evaluate original contribution relative to AI-Newton/CoSR/Wang–Buehler/causal identification/anti-sycophancy before paper drafting.

## Disclosure tiers: public vs private vs restricted

**PUBLIC — acceptable after source/license checking:**
- high-level founder research problem (without verbatim private conversations), public literature map with verified references, published criticisms and uncertainty;
- method and falsifiable null/negative controls, sanitized prompts and synthetic datasets, reproducible evaluation metrics and independently verified results;
- limitations, reproducibility details, version/commit of tested model and cost estimates.
- Materials explicitly marked *research proposal*, not experimental results, until actual runs occur.

**PRIVATE RESEARCH WORKSPACE — not the public ACSS repo:**
- speculative novelty ranking or sensitive personal early idea notes if not yet selected for disclosure; unreleased drafts; private researcher correspondence;
- non-public performance evaluation involving confidential finance strategies or original nonlicensed financial datasets;
- private model tokens/API keys and raw exchanges with personal data.

**RESTRICTED / DO NOT PUBLISH without distinct authorization:**
- Bloombase proprietary information, customer names/architecture, confidential agreements/data and employee information;
- personal portfolio holdings, trades, algorithmic execution logic, financial account details;
- user-identifiable medical or other sensitive information; proprietary or restricted third-party model/data content.
- material misrepresenting invented results, author relationships, legal governance, priority or actual AI consciousness.

**Critical disclosure caveat:** A GitHub Draft PR on a public repository is **public**, not confidential. Avoid publishing a detailed potentially patentable implementation until inventor/IP goals and disclosure trade-offs have been explicitly reviewed. Public preprints may affect patent filing timing depending on jurisdiction; not legal advice.

## Working with external contributors

**Start alone:** bibliographic matrix, research questions, synthetic data generators, grounded scoring code and reproducible local model setup. Use Mac local-only inference; retain hardware/model/quantization info and the fact that prompt variants do not update foundation weights.

**Then ask for scientific criticism:** causal representation / identifiability, symbolic-regression/scientific ML, measurement and statistical inference. Share public-safe problem card, benchmarks and failure criteria. No unapproved emails, names as reviewers, collaborator claims or coauthorship promises.

**Later collaborative tasks:** richer multimodal/embodied data, trained subtraction/ablation, external replication and scientific discovery benchmarks. Keep governance independent from commercial sponsors.

**Never conflate Paper 1 and the present program:** Paper 1 T1–T25 and Zenodo remain frozen. Current research is problem-first; no Paper 2 title or submission approved.
