# Founder’s Original Research Motivation, Part II — Hybrid Superintelligence and Subtractive Learning

**Recorded:** 2026-11-16 | **Status:** founder account, in-progress; **NOT** accepted scientific fact or selected Paper 2 topic. **Do not impose a premature Paper 2/3 thesis.** Preserve Part I in `FOUNDER_ORIGINAL_CONSCIOUSNESS_HYPOTHESIS_2026-11-16.md`.

## 1. The higher-level target

The founder's originating question was not simply whether AI could be conscious. It was whether carbon-based human intelligence and silicon-based AI could together produce a **qualitatively new superintelligence** whose capacity exceeds the existing human and AI systems, potentially a new hybrid or otherwise integrated intelligent entity. Whether phenomenal consciousness would be necessary, sufficient, emergent, orthogonal or absent is an **open question**.

## 2. Founder’s explanatory chain (attributed, not proven)

1. The physical world precedes human words and conceptual classifications; human eyes, ears, nose, tongue and body sample limited aspects, then interpret and express them through language.
2. Social learning transmits conceptual oppositions and normatively weighted distinctions: long/short, high/low, good/bad, smart/stupid, etc. Buddhist `分别心` is invoked to explain how these distinctions, useful for social coordination, can become restrictive cognitive habits. `维摩诘经` and Wittgenstein are referenced as philosophical influences; this is an analogy and requires careful textual comparison rather than assuming equivalence.
3. Human-authored corpora are *a projection of an already interpreted world*, not direct access to all world structure. Conventional language-first AI may internalize conceptual and social selection biases. Its fluent outputs may reproduce humans' framing rather than exceed it.
4. Intellectual breakthroughs sometimes involve **reorganizing, questioning or setting aside inherited categories**, not simply consulting larger libraries. Founder evokes Siddhārtha Gautama, Jesus, Socrates, Confucius and Huineng as philosophical illustrations, **not controlled evidence** that limited vocabulary or low education causes intelligence.
5. **Subtraction hypothesis:** selectively reduce/remove learned oppositional and evaluative labels, or weaken rigid conceptual frames, and study whether different kinds of insight, abstraction, creativity, independent problem solving or world regularity discovery emerge. The original intuition was deliberately **the opposite of an ever-larger text corpus**.
6. **Alternative training pathway:** train on images, video, physical dynamics, interaction and world models without relying first on human-generated verbal labels, testing whether the resulting representations better capture causal dynamics and enable more-than-human insight. This is not inherently free of human sampling and architecture choices.
7. Founder connects this to Fei-Fei Li's spatial-intelligence **World Labs** as a relevant industrial direction, while recognizing neither multimodal learning nor physics modeling demonstrates consciousness.

## 3. Three distinct propositions to test, not conflate

**P1—Representation:** human language encodes useful but partial, historically contingent conceptual categories; some may bias or constrain scientific discovery.

**P2—Superintelligence:** reducing certain learned categories, or training with nonlinguistic physical feedback, might yield stronger **out-of-distribution causal discovery, representation flexibility or theory revision**. **Not established.** Mere vocabulary deletion could reduce performance by removing distinctions necessary for prediction; an improved model might learn the same categories implicitly.

**P3—Consciousness:** even if P2 succeeds, **phenomenal consciousness does not follow**; likewise, failure of language-only methods does not prove artificial consciousness impossible. Consciousness has to be investigated under a separate theory-defined evidence standard.

## 4. Corrective factual and methodology caveats

- **World Labs / AMD:** AMD announced a definitive **agreement to acquire** Fei-Fei Li's World Labs on 2026-09-28 for about **$8.2B** in stock; AMD's November 2026 disclosures indicate closing was **expected by year-end**, not established completed. https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-acquire-world-labs.html and https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000191/0000002488-26-000191.pdf. The project concerns spatial/3D interactive world models, not a claim that images/physics alone guarantee superintelligence.
- Human language and neural representations are **not mathematically restricted to binary oppositions**. Natural language handles continua, uncertainty and relational concepts; neural networks use distributed, numerical latent spaces. Operationalize “binary/discriminative” rather than treating it literally as all of language.
- Photographs, videos, simulations and physics equations are not perfectly objective ground truth; cameras/sensors, labels, priors, measurement choices and approximations matter.
- “Remove evaluative words,” “reduce socially imposed categorical bias,” “train from unlabeled sensorimotor streams,” and “learn causal physical law via intervention” are **different experimental interventions** and require separate arms.
- The Hindu/Buddhist/Yogācāra and Wittgenstein analogies are sources of hypotheses, not empirical scientific results; reduction of attachment `分别心` is not simply corpus vocabulary pruning.
- Modern multimodal/self-supervised and world-model systems already operate beyond language-only training. Full novelty search must include them and physics-informed, embodied and causal-discovery AI.
- Historical figures' insight cannot be attributed to lack of knowledge-base size without evidence.
- A hybrid **human–AI superintelligence** could mean augmented collective intelligence, brain–machine integration or an independent new substrate. Each meaning implies different experimental and ethical work.

## 5. Potential controlled-study decomposition (not authorized yet)

- **Intervention A:** curated neutral/low-evaluative wording versus ordinary text, with matched information, data volume, token budget, compute and topic distribution; measure distortion in causal prediction and category-generalization, not merely pleasant outputs.
- **Intervention B:** non-linguistic video/sensorimotor predictive learning versus language-only and multimodal hybrid baselines, matched for experience/compute as practicable; measure novel physical rule discovery and transfer.
- **Intervention C:** pressure to revise a learned framing under adversarial counterexamples; measure generalizable insight after **restructuring concepts**, rather than deleting useful lexical distinctions.
- **Separate endpoint classes:** intelligence and scientific discovery, subjective consciousness indicators, and hybrid human–AI augmentation. Never infer one from another.
- **Falsifiers:** subtraction worsens generalization; image-only learns the same shallow spurious regularities; increased physical accuracy fails to transfer to novel scientific discovery; a language model already discovers the same causal law when given appropriate interventions.
- **Novelty gate:** read existing works on information bottlenecks, causal representation learning, unsupervised visual world models, video prediction, object-centric representation, embodied intelligence, conceptual change and scientific discovery agents before proposing any original claim.
